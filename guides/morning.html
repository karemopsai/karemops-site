Your morning, on autopilot.

By Karem AlMoayyad24 Oct 2026Free guide

The Claude Code skill that runs your daily briefing before you sit down. Inbox triaged with replies drafted, the news that matters to your niche, and your top priorities on the screen.

Claude Code
> mkdir -p ~/.claude/skills/morning

What this is

Your first hour decides your day. Most people spend it the same way: 40 unread emails, 12 Twitter tabs, no clear top 3. By 10am they're already reacting instead of executing. The move isn't more discipline. Discipline runs out. The move is to hand the first hour off to a skill that does the boring parts before you open your laptop.

/morning is a Claude Code skill you install once. One command runs four modules: email triage with drafted replies, news for your niche, your top 3 priorities from your calendar and git commits, and yesterday's account stats. Run it manually or schedule it. It hands you a briefing.

What's inside

  • 01Create the skill folder
  • 02Paste the starter skill into SKILL.md
  • 03Check it's installed
  • 04Ask Claude to explain it
  • 05Swap the news for your niche
  • 06Run it manually for 3 mornings
  • 07Schedule it
  • 084 copy-paste prompts: Ecom operator news, Real estate agent news, Fitness coach news, B2B founder news
Heads up: Don't schedule it on day one. Run it manually for 3 mornings and fix anything wrong until the output is exactly what you want. The stats module returns "not configured" until you wire up a stats source.
