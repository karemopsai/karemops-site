01 · What this is

What you're setting up.

On a sale day the same 10 questions arrive 50 times: is it on all sizes, do you ship here, when does the code end. Every slow reply is a sale that goes somewhere else.

Claude doesn't read your Instagram DMs, so the setup splits the work. Claude writes the answers from your sale details. Meta Business Suite sends the common ones by itself. For everything else, you paste the DM into Claude and send its draft.

You need

  • A Claude account (any plan; Projects are on Free too)
  • Your Instagram business account or Facebook Page connected to Meta Business Suite
  • Your Black Friday details: prices, the code, dates, shipping, returns
  • 10 real replies you've sent customers before (for your voice)
Heads up: Meta's keyword replies only fire on an exact match and are case-sensitive, and they wait 15 minutes in case you reply yourself. If you've switched on Meta Business Agent, Meta pauses away messages and instant replies. Check every price and date in the answers before Friday.

02 · The steps

Set it up, step by step.

01

Make a Claude Project for the sale

Go to claude.ai/projects, click + New Project and name it Black Friday. Upload one file with your sale prices, the code and when it ends, shipping, and returns. Add 10 replies you've sent before, so the answers sound like you.

Write the answers before anyone asks

03

Put the top ones in Meta Business Suite

In Business Suite, open Inbox, then automations. Use the Frequently asked questions template for the top questions, and Custom keywords (desktop only) for words like "shipping" or "code". Keywords match exactly and are case-sensitive, so add the spellings people use. Automations work across Messenger, Instagram and WhatsApp accounts you've connected.

Paste in anything that doesn't fit

03 · Copy and paste

Your prompts.

PROMPT1. The 10 answers

Using my sale file and my past replies, write the 10 questions customers will DM me most this week, and a reply to each in my voice, under 300 characters. Use only facts from the sale file.

PROMPT2. Answer this DM

A customer sent this:
[paste the DM]

Draft my reply from the sale file, in my voice, under 300 characters. If the answer isn't in the file, tell me what's missing.

PROMPT3. The keyword list

For each of the 10 questions, list the words a customer would type in a DM, including short and misspelled versions (ship, shiping, delivery). Max 5 per question.

PROMPT4. Friday night recap

Here are the DMs I answered by hand today:
[paste them]

Which questions came up most that my FAQs didn't cover? Write a reply for each so I can add them for Saturday.
