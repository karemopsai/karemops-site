01 · What this is

What you're setting up.

In May 2026, Anthropic dropped 10 finance agent templates. Not chatbot prompts. Actual agent systems, wired into the same data feeds hedge funds pay six figures a year for: FactSet, S&P Global, Moody's, the London Stock Exchange Group. Seven of the ten are built for analysts, auditors and back-office teams (Pitch Builder, Meeting Preparer, Valuation Reviewer, GL Reconciler, Month-End Closer, Statement Auditor, KYC Screener).

The other three are the retail-investor stack: the Market Researcher watches every stock you own, the Model Builder builds a full financial model in Excel from scratch, and the Earnings Reviewer reads 90-minute earnings calls in seconds. No code, no terminal, no jargon. If you can copy-paste a link, you can run this.

You need

  • A paid Claude plan: Pro, Max, Team, or Enterprise (free tier won't cut it)
  • Claude on the web at claude.ai or the desktop app
  • Your list of tickers
Heads up: I'm not a financial advisor and this isn't advice. These are research tools, not oracles. They can't predict the future, and the model's assumptions (growth rate, margins, discount rate) drive the answer. Open the assumptions tab, verify any number your decision rests on against the actual SEC filing, and talk to a licensed advisor before making real money moves.

02 · The steps

Set it up, step by step.

01

Open Claude and go to Customize

Open Claude on the web at claude.ai or the desktop app. Bottom-left sidebar, click Customize.

02

Install the Finance plugin

Under Personal Plugins, hit the + button. Click Browse Plugins, scroll until you see Finance, and add it. All 10 skills are now live in Claude.

03

Add the Market Researcher from GitHub

Back in Personal Plugins, hit + again. Click Create Plugin, then Add Marketplace, then Add from a repository. Paste this link:

github.com/anthropics/financial-services/market-researcher

04

Add the Model Builder and Earnings Reviewer

Do the exact same thing two more times with these two links. That's the whole install.

github.com/anthropics/financial-services/model-builder
github.com/anthropics/financial-services/earnings-reviewer
05

Open a new chat and describe what you want

Claude picks the right agent automatically. These agents run in Claude.ai (web), Cowork (desktop) and Claude Code (terminal). Same agents, same capabilities. Pro move: ask Claude to run your portfolio briefing every Monday at 7am so you show up to a report, not a firehose.

06

Give it your thesis and ask for the bear case

"Analyze NVDA" is a lazy prompt. Tell it why you own the stock and ask whether the data still supports that. Claude defaults to balanced, so if you want reasons to pass, say it out loud: give me the strongest argument for not owning this right now. Paste your whole portfolio in one message instead of one stock at a time.

07

Always ask for the Excel, then verify

With the Model Builder, always request the .xlsx export. A model you can open and tweak beats a summary you can't audit. Claude will get 95% of it right, but if a specific number drives your decision, open the actual SEC filing and check it.

03 · Copy and paste

Your prompts.

PROMPTMarket Researcher: Monday morning briefing

Here's my portfolio: [tickers]. Give me the Monday morning
briefing. What moved last week, what news dropped, any rating
changes, and, this is the important part, flag anything that
would make me reconsider owning any of these.

PROMPTMarket Researcher: one-stock deep dive

Give me a full report on [ticker]. Business, latest earnings,
analyst consensus, biggest risks, what the bulls are saying,
what the bears are saying, and whether the current price makes
sense given the numbers.

PROMPTMarket Researcher: sector map

I'm thinking about the [sector] space. Map out the top 5-7
players. Who leads, who's gaining, who's dying. If I had to
pick one for a 2-year hold, who would it be and why.

PROMPTModel Builder: full build

Build me a complete financial model for [ticker] in Excel.
3 years of history, 5 years of projections. Include a DCF, a
comp table, and sensitivity tables. Export it as .xlsx so I
can download it.

PROMPTModel Builder: quick fair-value check

What's [ticker] actually worth? Build a fast DCF using current
consensus estimates. Show me the implied share price under
three cases, bull, base, bear.

PROMPTModel Builder: two-stock face-off

I'm deciding between [ticker 1] and [ticker 2]. Build a
side-by-side model, revenue growth, margins, valuation
multiples, implied upside. Tell me which one is cheaper for
what you're getting.

PROMPTEarnings Reviewer: one-call review

Review the latest earnings call for [ticker]. Give me: the
numbers vs expectations, what management said about [specific
thing you care about], what changed in their guidance, and
anything I should be worried about.

PROMPTEarnings Reviewer: the thesis test

I own [ticker] because I believe [your reason]. Read the
latest earnings call and tell me straight, does what
management said support that thesis or undermine it?
Be specific.

PROMPTEarnings Reviewer: earnings-season sweep

This week, [ticker 1], [ticker 2], [ticker 3] all reported.
Give me a one-paragraph summary of each. Who beat, who missed,
whose guidance was ugly. Rank them from most bullish to most
bearish based on the *quality* of what they said, not just
the beat.

PROMPTChain all three: full due diligence

I'm thinking about buying [ticker]. Run full due diligence:
Step 1. Market Researcher: news, analyst view, competitive
position, risks.
Step 2. Model Builder: build me a DCF with bull/base/bear
cases and export the Excel.
Step 3. Earnings Reviewer: read the last call. Any red

Then give me your final call: buy, wait, or pass. And be
honest, if the numbers don't hold up, say so.
