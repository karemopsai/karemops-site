01 · What this is

What you're setting up.

Meta dropped an official MCP server for Meta Ads. Translation: Claude and ChatGPT plug directly into your ad account and do things, not just talk about them. It lives at mcp.facebook.com/ads, it's in public beta, and setup takes about 5 minutes.

You stop being the person clicking 47 buttons in Ads Manager. You start being the person typing one sentence and watching the work happen. From a chat you can spin up full campaigns (paused, so you review before going live), pull real performance data like ROAS, CPA, CTR, frequency and breakdowns, manage product catalogs and fix feed issues, diagnose Pixel + CAPI signal quality, and search Meta's Help Center when something breaks.

You need

  • A Facebook account with admin access to your Meta Business Portfolio
  • An active Meta ad account inside that portfolio
  • Claude (Pro, Max, Team, Enterprise) or ChatGPT (Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, Edu). Claude Free works but caps you at one custom connector. ChatGPT Free can't do custom connectors at all, you need Plus minimum.
  • 5 minutes
Heads up: It's in beta. Don't run it on a client's account without their go-ahead. Every write action asks for your confirmation before it fires, so nothing happens behind your back.

02 · The steps

Set it up, step by step.

01

Grab the MCP URL

Nothing to download. Meta hosts the server. You just point your AI at it. Then pick your tool below.

https://mcp.facebook.com/ads
02

Claude: add the connector

About 2 minutes, on Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise. Go to claude.ai → profile pic (bottom-left) → Settings → Connectors. Scroll down → Add custom connector. Name it Meta Ads, paste the URL, click Add. On Team / Enterprise only Owners add the connector to the org. Each member then logs in with their own Facebook. No shared credentials, no security mess.

Name: Meta Ads
URL:  https://mcp.facebook.com/ads
03

Claude: connect your Meta account and turn it on

Hit Connect next to Meta Ads. The Facebook OAuth window opens, log in. Pick the Business Portfolio + ad accounts you want Claude to touch and approve. Green "Connected" badge = you're in. Then open a new chat → + button → Connectors → toggle Meta Ads on and start prompting.

04

ChatGPT: turn on Developer Mode

About 4 minutes, on Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise or Edu. ChatGPT makes you flip on Developer Mode first, so slightly more friction. Plus or Pro: chatgpt.com → profile pic → Settings → Connectors → scroll to Advanced settings → toggle Developer mode on, read the warning, accept. Business / Enterprise / Edu: an admin enables it under Workspace Settings → Permissions & Roles → Connected Data Developer mode.

05

ChatGPT: create the connector and authenticate

Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector (or Create on workspaces). Set the name, server URL and OAuth auth, then click Create. Allow popups on chatgpt.com (otherwise the OAuth window dies silently), log into Facebook with your admin account, pick your ad accounts and approve.

Name:       Meta Ads
Server URL: https://mcp.facebook.com/ads
Auth:       OAuth
06

ChatGPT: turn it on in your chat

New chat → + → hover More → toggle Developer Mode on → select Meta Ads from the tools menu. Two gotchas everyone hits. One: ChatGPT auto-kills memory when Developer Mode is on. Intentional, it keeps the connector from polluting your other chats. Two: you have to tell ChatGPT to use it. Start prompts with "Use the Meta Ads MCP to…" or it'll ignore the connector and just guess.
