01 · What this is

What you're setting up.

One tool (Late) connects all your socials. One MCP install lets Claude Code talk to Late. One skill (/post-content) runs the whole publish loop end to end: transcribe, caption per platform, schedule, ship. You stop logging into five apps. You drop a video file, Claude transcribes it, writes platform-specific captions (because TikTok ≠ LinkedIn), and posts. Done in under two minutes.

Once it's connected you can post one video to IG Reels, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts and X from the same prompt, get a custom caption per platform, schedule or queue or fire immediately, re-post old winners with a fresh caption in one line, and pull post analytics back into Claude to brief your next video. It replaces the 4 hours a week of cross-posting and re-formatting captions.

You need

  • A Late account (free tier works to test)
  • Your social accounts ready to auth: IG (Business or Creator), TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, X
  • IG on a Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook page (2 minutes in the IG app if you're still on personal)
  • Claude Code installed
  • One short-form video you actually want to post (test run = real run)
Heads up: Late actually publishes. Wrong caption, broken link, scheduled at 3 AM, that's all live. Always preview the draft Claude generates before approving the publish step.

02 · The steps

Set it up, step by step.

01

Get a Late account

One tool that posts to every platform you care about. Go to getlate.dev and sign up with Google or email. Create a workspace, one per brand, this is where every post lives. Then Settings, Developer, generate a personal API key. Copy it. You'll paste it once in step 3.

02

Connect your socials

Inside Late, hit Connect Account and auth each platform once, top to bottom. Then verify: Late dashboard, Accounts tab, 5 green dots. If any are red or "Re-auth needed," fix it now. The MCP won't recover later if a platform is half-connected.

1. Instagram   (Business/Creator account, needs a linked FB page)
2. TikTok      (Personal or Business both work)
3. LinkedIn    (Personal profile OR company page, your call)
4. YouTube     (Channel auth via Google)
5. X           (OAuth, takes ~10 seconds)
03

Wire Late into Claude Code

One command. Claude can now read your Late workspace and create posts. No restart needed, MCPs hot-load. To confirm, open any Claude Code session and ask "list my Late accounts". Expect 5 platforms back. If you get zero, the key didn't paste cleanly. Run claude mcp list and re-add.

claude mcp add late
# Paste the API key from Step 01 when prompted.

03 · Copy and paste

Your prompts.

PROMPTPost now with your own hook

/post-content video.mp4
Use this hook for all 5 captions: "I stopped paying a VA $800/mo. Here's why."

PROMPTSchedule for tomorrow 9 AM, skip X

/post-content video.mp4 schedule="tomorrow 09:00"
platforms="ig,tiktok,linkedin,youtube"
Stagger posts by 5 minutes so the algos don't cross-pollinate.

PROMPTRepost your winners

Find my top 3 Reels from the last 30 days.
Rewrite the hooks. Re-queue to Late for next week, one per day at 10 AM.
