01 · What this is

What you're setting up.

Zernio (the social media API formerly called Late) connects your social accounts once and gives Claude Code a hosted MCP server for posting, scheduling and analytics across 15+ networks.

You give Claude the video and the idea. It writes a native caption for each platform, shows you the drafts, then posts or schedules them. No scheduler app, no copy-pasting the same caption five times.

You need

  • A Zernio account (the first 2 connected accounts are free)
  • Claude Code installed
  • Business or Creator Instagram (linked to a Facebook page), plus TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube and X logins
Heads up: Zernio really publishes. A wrong caption, a broken link or a 3 AM schedule goes live on every platform at once. Always ask for drafts first and approve before Claude posts. Beyond 2 accounts Zernio charges per connected account, so check current pricing.

02 · The steps

Set it up, step by step.

01

Create your Zernio account

Sign up at zernio.com/signup. The first 2 connected accounts are free, enough to test the whole flow before you connect all five.

02

Connect your socials

In the Zernio dashboard, connect each account. Instagram needs a Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook page.

1. Instagram   (Business/Creator account, needs a linked FB page)
2. TikTok      (Personal or Business both work)
3. LinkedIn    (Personal profile OR company page, your call)
4. YouTube     (Channel auth via Google)
5. X           (OAuth, takes ~10 seconds)
03

Create an API key

Dashboard, then API Keys, then create a key. Keep it private: anyone with it can post as you.

Connect Zernio to Claude Code

03 · Copy and paste

Your prompts.

PROMPTDraft captions for every platform

Here's my video: [file]. The idea: [one line].
Write a native caption for Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts and X.
Keep my voice, end each with [CTA]. Show me all five drafts. Don't post anything yet.

PROMPTPost now, after you approve

Post the approved drafts through Zernio to all five accounts now.
List each platform with the post link when done.

PROMPTSchedule and skip one

Schedule the approved drafts for tomorrow at 9 AM my time on Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn and YouTube.
Skip X. Confirm the schedule back to me before you send it.
