01 · What this is

What you're setting up.

Most owners buy AI tools one at a time and none of them talk to each other. A system is different: ideas go in one end, finished work comes out the other, and the same place holds every step. Your team still runs it. The system decides what to make and drafts it, so nobody starts from a blank page.

All three are built with Claude Code. Start with the one that matches your biggest leak: no content going out, ads you can't read, or numbers you only see at the end of the month.

You need

  • Claude Code (paid Claude plan) to build them
  • An Airtable account (the free plan is enough to start)
  • N8N (cloud or self-hosted) to move work between steps
  • Access to your Meta ad account, for systems 2 and 3
  • One person on your team who will run it every week
Heads up: A system is only as good as what you feed it. If nobody approves the scripts or checks the dashboard, it stops working in 3 weeks. Pick the owner before you build. And keep a person on every ad launch: Claude drafts, a person presses publish.

The 3 systems

What each one does

01

System 1: Content

An Airtable base holds every idea, script and post. Claude Code pulls what competitors posted and turns the winners into angles. It writes scripts and carousel outlines in your voice. N8N moves each approved piece into a publishing queue. Your team films, checks and posts. Needs from you: a voice doc, 3 competitors, and 30 minutes a week to approve.

02

System 2: Ads

First, your Meta ad account gets cleaned up: one campaign structure, clear names, and the pixel and Conversions API checked. Then a Claude Code engine scrapes competitor ads from the Meta Ad Library, writes ad briefs from what's working, and preps launches. Needs from you: access to the ad account, your offer, and a person who makes the creatives.

03

System 3: Numbers

One page that shows where your funnel breaks, refreshed every week. Ad spend, leads, calls and sales sit side by side, so you see which step leaks. The data lives in Supabase and the page is built with Claude Code. Needs from you: access to your ad account and wherever your leads and sales live (GoHighLevel, Airtable, Shopify).

04

Build one first

No content going out? Build System 1. Spending on ads but can't tell what works? Build System 3, then 2. Never build all three at once. Each one needs 2 to 4 weeks of use before it runs smoothly.

Give it an owner

[paste them]

Group them by angle, tell me which angles show up most, and write 3 ad briefs for my offer: [your offer]. Hook, angle, what the visual shows, call to action.

PROMPT3. Numbers: the one-page dashboard

In this folder are 3 CSV exports: ads.csv, leads.csv, sales.csv. Build a one-page HTML dashboard: spend, leads, cost per lead, calls, sales, by week. Highlight the step where the drop is biggest. Tell me which columns you matched between files.
