01 · What this is

What you're setting up.

Most creators film one idea, post it once, and pray. One idea can carry a month of content, you just never had the machine to unpack it.

Claude Code is that machine. You hand it a single idea and your voice. It writes 5 angles, 4 hooks for each angle, and 5 full scripts per hook. That's 100 shorts for IG Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, every one built to be filmed.

You need

  • Claude Code installed (or Claude desktop or any chat, the prompt works anywhere)
  • Your top 5 hooks that actually performed, copied from your best posts
  • A short writing sample in your voice (one caption or script you'd be proud of)
  • One idea. Steal one from a comment, a DM question you keep getting, or a competitor's best video.
Heads up: The prompt is only as good as what you feed it. Garbage voice sample in, generic scripts out. Give it your real hooks and a real writing sample or it sounds like every other AI script on the feed.

02 · The steps

Set it up, step by step.

01

Load your inputs

Gather three things before you paste the prompt. Your top 5 hooks: the first line from your 5 best-performing posts. A voice sample: one script or caption you'd actually post, pasted raw, imperfect grammar and all. Your one idea: one sentence, like "AI kills busywork" or "why most founders can't delegate." This is the 10 minutes that decides whether the output is fire or filler.

02

See how it hits 100

The prompt reframes your idea 5 ways (tutorial, contrarian take, story, shock/pattern-break, proof/results), writes 4 opening hooks per angle modeled on your top 5, then writes 5 full 30 to 40 second scripts per hook. 5 × 4 × 5 = 100. Same script cross-posts to IG Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

03

Run the master prompt

Paste this into Claude Code, fill in the three brackets with your inputs, and hit enter. If it stops early (long outputs sometimes pause), type continue and it picks up where it left off.

You are my short-form content writer. You study my voice and my
best hooks, then generate scripts that sound exactly like me.

MY VOICE SAMPLE:
[paste one caption or script in your voice, raw is fine]

MY TOP 5 HOOKS (these already worked):
[paste the first line from your 5 best posts]

MY IDEA:
[one sentence]

TASK: turn that ONE idea into 100 short-form scripts:
1. Write 5 distinct ANGLES on the idea:
   tutorial, contrarian take, story, shock/pattern-break, proof.
2. For EACH angle, write 4 different HOOKS (0-3s opening lines),
   modeled on the style of my top 5 hooks above.
3. For EACH hook, write 5 full SCRIPTS. Every script is:
   - 30-40 seconds spoken
   - structured: HOOK → 1-line context → the payoff → CTA
   - written in MY voice, not corporate AI voice
   - designed for a talking-head shot, cross-posted to
     IG Reels + TikTok + YouTube Shorts (same video everywhere)

RULES:
- No fluff, no "in today's video", no throat-clearing.
- Every hook must earn the next line. Cut anything skippable.
- Vary the hooks hard: question, bold claim, number, callout, story.
- End every script with a one-line CTA I can swap later.

OUTPUT: number them 1-100, grouped by angle then hook.
Start with angle 1, hook 1, scripts 1-5. Keep going to 100.

04

Cut it down to winners
