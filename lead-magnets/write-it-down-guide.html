01 · What this is

What you're setting up.

Most owners want the automation first: the follow-ups, the reports, the DM replies. Then it breaks on day 2, because nobody wrote down what the job is. Claude can't guess what's in your head, and neither can a new hire.

The fix is boring and it works. For every job you repeat, write what comes in, what you do with it and what goes out. The jobs you can write cleanly are the ones to hand off first, to Claude or to a person.

You need

  • A notes app or a Google Doc
  • 30 minutes for your first 10 jobs
  • A Claude account (any plan) for the gap check in step 4
  • Optional: a voice memo app, if talking is easier than typing
Heads up: Don't automate a job you haven't run by hand from the doc at least once. If you skip a step when you follow your own doc, the doc is wrong. Fix it before Claude or anyone else uses it.

02 · The steps

Set it up, step by step.

01

List the jobs you repeat

Go through last week: your sent emails, your DMs, your calendar. Write every job you did more than once. Answering price questions, sending invoices, booking calls, posting. Aim for 10 to 20.

02

Write 3 lines for each job

What comes in (a DM, an invoice, a form). What you do with it. What goes out (a reply, a file, a booking). If you can't write the 3 lines, the job isn't clear yet. Mark it and move on.

03

Fill in the one-page doc

For the 3 jobs that cost you the most time, fill in the full template below. One page each. Use your real tools by name: Gmail, Airtable, GoHighLevel, WhatsApp.

JOB: [name]
OWNER: [who does it today]
TRIGGER: [what starts it]
INPUT: [what arrives and where]
STEPS:
1. [first thing you do]
2. [next]
3. [next]
OUTPUT: [what leaves, to whom]
TOOLS: [apps you touch]
RULES: [prices, limits, what you never do]
DONE WHEN: [how you know it's finished]
WHEN IT GOES WRONG: [the usual problem and the fix]
04

Let Claude find the gaps

Paste one filled doc into Claude with the first prompt on the next page. It asks the questions a new hire would ask. Answer them in the doc, not in the chat.

05

Hand off the cleanest one

Rank your jobs with the last prompt. Hand off the one with the clearest doc first. Run it from the doc for a week, fix what breaks, then pick the next.
