01 · What this is

What you're setting up.

Fable 5 was pulled by US export controls, cleared June 30, and redeployed July 1 as a safeguarded version. Free-included on Pro, Max, and Team through July 7. After that it's still there, it just stops being free. It's the strongest model you can put your hands on today, and it's expensive to run: roughly $10 in / $50 out per million tokens, about twice Opus.

So the game is simple: point it at hard, high-value work, not everyday chat. Reserve it for the jobs you'd normally break into pieces or hand to an expert. Build things once with it, then let a cheaper model run them on repeat. Don't waste it on tasks Sonnet or Opus already handle fine.

You need

  • A paid plan: Pro, Max, or Team (or API credits). This is not a free-plan perk.
  • Claude Code, Cursor, or the Claude app, wherever you already work
  • One hard, real problem worth pointing a frontier model at
Heads up: Don't ask it to "show its reasoning" or "explain its thinking" step by step. On the current model that can trip a safety classifier and quietly drop you to Opus 4.8. Some requests (cyber, bio/chem, some coding) fall back to Opus 4.8 the same way. Ask for the answer and the work, not a running narration of its thought process.

02 · The steps

Set it up, step by step.

01

Give it your hardest problems

Start at the top of your difficulty range. Fable is built for the jobs you'd normally split up or outsource, so hand it the whole thing. Easy tasks waste it.

02

Keep your prompts short

Over-engineered prompts actually degrade the output. State the goal and the constraints, then stop. Let the model do the reasoning instead of scripting every step.

03

Tell it why you're asking

Give it the intent, not just the request. Knowing the "why" lets it make better judgment calls on the parts you didn't spell out.

04

Let it run, and let it self-verify

Fable can work for hours on one goal and check its own work with subagents. Give it room to run instead of interrupting every few minutes.

05

Spend it once on durable assets

Fable burns credits fast. The best use of the free window is building durable assets once that you then run on a cheaper model forever: your brand system (#6), your second-brain rebuild (#7), your auto-repurposing engine (#10). Spend Fable once. Reuse the result cheap.
