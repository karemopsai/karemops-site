01 · What this is

What you're setting up.

Most owners use Claude for one thing: rewriting an email. The map shows the rest. Every job on it uses a feature Anthropic lists on its own help pages: web search, file creation (Word, Excel, PDF), Projects, and the Gmail, Calendar and Drive connectors.

Don't run all 18 this week. Pick the department that eats most of your week, run its 3 jobs every day for 7 days, and only then move to the next one. That's how a job becomes a habit and not a demo.

You need

  • A Claude account. File creation, Projects and the Google connectors are on every plan, Free included
  • Claude on the web or the Claude Desktop app
  • Your Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive (for the admin and customer jobs)
  • A paid plan only for job 18 (the dashboard), which runs in Claude Code
Heads up: Claude asks before it sends an email by default. Keep that on. Read every proposal, reply and figure before it reaches a customer: Claude can misread a PDF or a number. On a Team or Enterprise plan, the owner has to switch on web search, file creation and the connectors for the workspace first (Organization settings).

02 · The steps

Set it up, step by step.

01

Turn on file creation

In Claude, open Settings, then Capabilities, and switch on Code execution and file creation (in the newer layout it's called Create and edit files). This is what lets Claude hand you a Word doc, an Excel sheet or a PDF. Files can be up to 30MB each.

02

Connect Gmail, Calendar and Drive

In a chat, click the + button, hover over Connectors and switch on Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive. The first time, Claude asks you to sign in with your Google account. You can switch each one off from the same menu.

03

Switch on web search when you need it

For jobs 1 and 6, click + in the chat and pick Web search. A checkmark shows it's on. Claude then reads live pages and gives you the links it used.

04

Make one Project per department

Go to claude.ai/projects, click + New Project and name it (Sales, Marketing). Click Set project instructions and paste 3 lines about your business: what you sell, who buys, how you talk. Add your price list and a few past emails to the project knowledge. Free plans can have 5 projects.

05

Pick one branch for 7 days

Choose the department that takes most of your week. Run its 3 prompts from the next pages every day for 7 days. When they feel normal, move to the next branch.
