01 · What this is

What you're setting up.

Writing a proposal after a call takes most owners an evening. And the costly part isn't the time: it's the extra the client asked for on the call that never made it onto the price list, so you do it for free.

Claude can read your notes and your price list and write the proposal as a Word doc, on any plan. The last line of the prompt is the one that pays: it tells Claude to flag anything you didn't price, so you see it before you send.

You need

  • A Claude account with file creation switched on (Settings, then Capabilities)
  • Your price list (a PDF, a sheet or plain text)
  • One past proposal you liked
  • Your notes or the transcript from the call
Heads up: Read the flags and every price before you send. Claude uses only what's in your price list if you tell it to, but it can still misread a line. Never send a proposal you haven't read top to bottom.

02 · The steps

Set it up, step by step.

01

Make a Proposals Project

Go to claude.ai/projects and click + New Project. Name it Proposals.

02

Add your price list and a past proposal

In the project knowledge panel, click + and upload your price list and one past proposal you liked. Claude uses the first for numbers and the second for layout and tone.

03

Set the instructions

Click Set project instructions and paste this. It applies to every chat in the project.

You write proposals for [business name].
Use only the prices in the price list file. Never invent a price, a discount or a deadline.
Follow the layout of the past proposal file.
Always end with a list called FLAGS: anything the client asked for that isn't on my price list, and anything in the notes that's unclear.

04

After every call, one prompt

Start a new chat in the project, paste or upload your call notes, and send the main prompt from the next page. You get a Word doc and a list of flags.

05

Price the flags, then send

Go through the FLAGS list. Add a price, cut the item, or ask the client. Then ask Claude to update the doc and send it yourself.

03 · Copy and paste

Your prompts.

PROMPT1. The proposal

Turn these call notes into a proposal. Use only the prices in my price list. Word doc. Flag anything I didn't price.

[paste call notes]

PROMPT2. Add the flagged item

Add [item] at [price] to the proposal, update the total and give me the new Word doc.

PROMPT3. The cover email

Write a short email to send with this proposal. Remind them of the one problem they told me about on the call and the next step. Under 100 words.

PROMPT4. Check before you send

Read the proposal like the client would. List anything unclear, any price that doesn't match the price list, and any promise I didn't make on the call.
